## Goal
Second Serve is a full-stack web application designed to reduce environmental food waste and alleviate local hunger by creating a real-time marketplace for surplus food. Using a MERN/PERN stack, the platform utilizes role-based authentication to connect two distinct user bases: donor businesses (like restaurants and bakeries) who log in to list perfectly good, unsold food items, and recipient organizations (like shelters and food banks) who log in to claim and track these donations. The backend manages live reservation states, item expiration countdowns, and localized mapping APIs, turning logistics management into a direct tool for community mutual aid.
